# 📊 Financial Sales Performance Dashboard — Excel

![Sales Performance Dashboard](sales_performance_dashboard.png)

## Project Overview

This project analyzes **1,800 financial sales transactions from 2024–2026** using Microsoft Excel.

I built an interactive dashboard using **PivotTables, PivotCharts, KPI cards, slicers, and a timeline** to understand:

- Overall sales and profitability
- Monthly sales trends
- Country and regional performance
- Product performance
- Sales channel performance
- High-margin and low-margin products

The goal was to turn raw financial data into a dashboard that makes business performance easy to understand.

## Key Insights

### 📈 Sales Performance

**2025 was the strongest year**, generating approximately **$12.71M in revenue** and **$5.99M in profit**.

Across all months, **July produced the highest revenue at $3.87M** and approximately **$1.79M in profit**.

This suggests stronger business performance during the middle of the year.

---

### 🌎 Country Performance

The **USA was the strongest market**, generating approximately:

- **$9.89M Revenue**
- **$4.53M Profit**

Canada followed closely with approximately **$9.71M in revenue**.

Australia had the **highest profit margin at 48.95%**, showing strong profitability even though its total sales were lower than the USA and Canada.

---

### 📍 Regional Performance

The **East region generated the highest revenue and profit**:

- Revenue: **$7.63M**
- Profit: **$3.55M**

The **West region had the highest profit margin at 46.96%**.

The **Central region had the lowest margin at 42.72%**, which may be worth further investigation.

---

### 📦 Product Performance

**Consulting was the strongest-performing product**.

It generated:

- **$10.73M Revenue**
- **$6.30M Profit**
- **58.70% Profit Margin**

Implementation was also a strong performer with approximately **$7.47M in revenue** and **$3.91M in profit**.

One of the most interesting findings was **Office Bundle**.

Office Bundle had the:

- **Highest number of transactions — 237**
- **Highest units sold — 5,032**
- But only an **11.29% profit margin**

This shows that **high sales volume does not always mean high profitability**.

---

### 🛒 Sales Channel Performance

**Direct Sales was the strongest sales channel**, generating:

- **$11.33M Revenue**
- **$5.15M Profit**
- **590 transactions**

However, **Retail had the highest profit margin at 47.03%**.

This means Direct Sales leads in total business volume, while Retail performs slightly better from a margin perspective.

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Focus on high-profit products

**Consulting and Implementation** generate strong revenue and margins, so they should remain important parts of the product strategy.

### 2. Review Office Bundle profitability

Office Bundle sells frequently but has a much lower profit margin than other products.

The business should review:

- Pricing
- Discounts
- Product cost
- Operating expenses

### 3. Protect strong markets

The **USA and Canada** are the largest markets and should remain key priorities.

At the same time, markets such as **Australia** may provide growth opportunities because of their stronger margins.

### 4. Investigate regional margin differences

The Central region has the lowest margin among the regions. Further analysis could identify whether pricing, costs, product mix, or discounts are responsible.

### 5. Use monthly trends for planning

Strong months such as **July and August** can help with future sales forecasting, inventory planning, staffing, and budgeting.

## Calculated Metrics

```text
Revenue = Units Sold × Unit Price × (1 - Discount %)

COGS = Units Sold × Unit Cost

Profit = Revenue - COGS - Operating Expense

Profit Margin % = Profit / Revenue

Revenue Variance = Revenue - Budget Revenue
```
